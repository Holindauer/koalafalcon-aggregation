\begin{abstract}

SNARK-based lattice signature aggregation faces the challenge of non-native arithmetization 
to encode ring-arithmetic at the coefficient level over SNARK typical fields. In this work, we 
present KoalaFalcon, 
a variant of the Falcon signature scheme over the KoalaBear prime that uses Poseidon for 
message hashes. We present two parameter sets for KoalaFalcon achieving a UF-CMA bit security of 
117.98 and 256 respectively using the techniques of CoreFalcon+, arguing
the prime modulus does not affect the applicability of the security reduction
so long as the intended ring-algebraic properties are met, and the signature sampler 
standard deviation is above a lattice specific smoothing parameter. Adherence to these 
criteria results in instances with concretely hard estimates for SIS based on the core-SVP 
methodology. 

\end{abstract}
